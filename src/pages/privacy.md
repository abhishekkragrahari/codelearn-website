*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit **GateShala** (https://www.gateshala.com/) and how it is used. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

GateShala does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to read the lessons.

## Information stored in your browser

The site saves two small settings in your browser's local storage: your colour theme (light or dark) and the programming language you last picked in code examples. These settings stay on your device, are never sent to us, and you can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

## Services provided by third parties

Some parts of the site use services run by other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you visited.

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the website and may keep server logs of visits.
- **Google Fonts** provides the fonts used on the site.
- **jsDelivr** delivers code-editor files used by the playground.
- **Wandbox** (wandbox.org) runs the code you choose to run in the playground. The code you type is sent to Wandbox only when you press Run, so do not put personal information in it.

## Advertising and cookies

GateShala may show advertisements served by **Google AdSense** to keep the site free.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To learn more, read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Children's information

GateShala is meant for students and learners of all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will remove it.

## Links to other websites

Lessons may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach us through the [contact page](contact.html).
