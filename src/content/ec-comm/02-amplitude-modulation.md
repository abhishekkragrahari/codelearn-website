**Amplitude modulation** varies the amplitude of a high-frequency carrier with the message. It is simple, and appears in GATE as power, bandwidth and receiver questions.

## Conventional AM (DSB-FC)

s(t) = A_c[1 + μ cos ω_m t] cos ω_c t

- **Modulation index** μ = A_m/A_c (must be ≤ 1 to avoid over-modulation). From the envelope: μ = (A_max − A_min)/(A_max + A_min).
- Spectrum: carrier at f_c and sidebands at f_c ± f_m. **Bandwidth = 2f_m** (2W for a message of bandwidth W).
- **Total power P_t = P_c(1 + μ²/2)**; sideband power = P_c μ²/2.
- **Power efficiency** η = μ²/(2 + μ²): at most **33.3%** (μ = 1).
- Multiple tones: μ_t = √(μ₁² + μ₂² + …).
- **Envelope detector:** simple; needs μ ≤ 1 and an RC time constant with 1/f_c ≪ RC ≪ 1/W.

## DSB-SC

s(t) = m(t) cos ω_c t: no carrier power wasted, bandwidth 2W. Needs **coherent (synchronous) detection**: multiply by a local carrier and low-pass filter. A phase error φ scales the output by cos φ (zero at 90°). Carrier recovery by squaring loop or Costas loop.

## SSB and VSB

- **SSB:** only one sideband, **bandwidth W**: saves bandwidth and power. Generated by filtering or the phase-shift (Hilbert transform) method.
- **VSB:** one sideband plus a vestige of the other; used for TV picture signals where the message has low-frequency content.

## Superheterodyne receiver

All stations are converted to a fixed **intermediate frequency (IF)** (455 kHz for AM broadcast).

- Local oscillator f_LO = f_s + f_IF (usually above the signal).
- **Image frequency** f_image = f_s + 2f_IF; it must be rejected by the RF stage.
- **Image rejection ratio** α = √(1 + Q²ρ²), with ρ = f_image/f_s − f_s/f_image.

## Worked example

A 100 W carrier is amplitude modulated with μ = 0.5.

- P_t = 100(1 + 0.125) = **112.5 W**; sideband power 12.5 W; efficiency = 12.5/112.5 = **11.1%**.

## Practice

```quiz
q: An AM wave has a maximum envelope of 15 V and a minimum of 5 V. What is the modulation index?
answer: 0.5
verify: (15 - 5) / (15 + 5)
explain: μ = (15 − 5)/(15 + 5) = 0.5.
```

```quiz
q: An AM broadcast receiver with IF = 455 kHz is tuned to 1000 kHz. What is the image frequency in kHz?
answer: 1910
verify: 1000 + 2 * 455
explain: f_image = f_s + 2f_IF = 1910 kHz.
```

```quiz
q: What is the maximum power efficiency of conventional AM (single tone), in percent? (Give 2 decimal places.)
answer: 33.33
verify: round(1 / (2 + 1) * 100, 2)
explain: η = μ²/(2 + μ²) = 1/3 at μ = 1.
```

```quiz
q: A DSB-SC signal is detected with a local carrier that has a 60° phase error. The output amplitude becomes what fraction of the correct value?
answer: 0.5
verify: round(__import__("math").cos(__import__("math").radians(60)), 6)
explain: The output scales by cos φ = cos 60° = 0.5.
```

```quiz
q: A 50 W carrier is amplitude modulated with μ = 0.8. What is the total transmitted power in W?
answer: 66
verify: round(50 * (1 + 0.8 ** 2 / 2), 6)
explain: P_t = 50(1 + 0.32) = 66 W.
```

```quiz
q: An AM broadcast receiver (IF 455 kHz) is tuned to 600 kHz. What is the local oscillator frequency in kHz?
answer: 1055
verify: 600 + 455
explain: f_LO = f_s + f_IF = 1055 kHz.
```
