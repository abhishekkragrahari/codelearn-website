Two schedules for GATE Electronics and Communication Engineering: a **6-month plan** (about 4–5 hours a day) and a **3-month plan** (about 7–8 hours a day). Both use the lessons of this track, [Engineering Mathematics](../em/index.html), [General Aptitude](../ga/index.html) and the [mock test](mock-test-1.html).

> **Note:** GATE 2027 is organised by IIT Madras ([gate2027.iitm.ac.in](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/)). Check the official brochure for the syllabus and dates.

## Subject order

Networks and signals are the base for control and communications; devices come before analog circuits.

1. [Networks](networks.html)
2. [Signals and systems](signals-systems.html)
3. [Electronic devices](electronic-devices.html)
4. [Analog circuits](analog-circuits.html)
5. [Digital circuits](digital-circuits.html)
6. [Control systems](control-systems.html)
7. [Communications](communications.html)
8. [Electromagnetics](electromagnetics.html)

## 6-month plan

| Weeks | Main subject | Side subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Networks | Engineering Mathematics: linear algebra, calculus |
| 4–6 | Signals and systems | Engineering Mathematics: Laplace, Fourier, complex variables |
| 7–8 | Electronic devices | General Aptitude: quantitative |
| 9–11 | Analog circuits | Engineering Mathematics: ODEs, probability |
| 12–13 | Digital circuits | General Aptitude: verbal and reasoning |
| 14–15 | Control systems | Revise networks and signals |
| 16–18 | Communications | Revise devices and analog |
| 19–20 | Electromagnetics | Engineering Mathematics: vector calculus |
| 21–23 | Revision with the [formula sheet](formula-sheet.html) | One previous year paper per week |
| 24–26 | Mocks: two per week, with mistake analysis | Weak topics only |

## Tips for electronics and communication

- **Signals and systems is everywhere**: Fourier and Laplace transforms reappear in control and communications.
- **Electromagnetics is formula heavy**: keep a separate page for transmission lines and waveguides.
- **Digital circuits give quick marks**: counters, multiplexers and K-maps are short questions.
- Use official previous year papers from the organising IIT's site; see [how to analyse them](../prep/previous-year-papers.html).
