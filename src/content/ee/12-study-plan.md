Two schedules for GATE Electrical Engineering: a **6-month plan** (about 4–5 hours a day) and a **3-month plan** (about 7–8 hours a day). Both use the lessons of this track, [Engineering Mathematics](../em/index.html), [General Aptitude](../ga/index.html) and the [mock test](mock-test-1.html).

> **Note:** GATE 2027 is organised by IIT Madras ([gate2027.iitm.ac.in](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/)). Check the official brochure for the syllabus and dates.

## Subject order

Electric circuits are used in almost every EE subject, so they come first. Signals and systems come before control systems; machines come before power systems.

1. [Electric circuits](electric-circuits.html)
2. [Electromagnetic fields](electromagnetic-fields.html)
3. [Signals and systems](signals-systems.html)
4. [Control systems](control-systems.html)
5. [Electrical machines](electrical-machines.html)
6. [Power systems](power-systems.html)
7. [Analog and digital electronics](analog-digital-electronics.html)
8. [Power electronics](power-electronics.html)
9. [Measurements](measurements.html)

## 6-month plan

| Weeks | Main subject | Side subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Electric circuits | Engineering Mathematics: linear algebra, calculus |
| 4 | Electromagnetic fields | Engineering Mathematics: vector calculus |
| 5–6 | Signals and systems | Engineering Mathematics: Laplace, Fourier, complex variables |
| 7–8 | Control systems | General Aptitude: quantitative |
| 9–11 | Electrical machines | Engineering Mathematics: ODEs, probability |
| 12–14 | Power systems | General Aptitude: verbal and reasoning |
| 15–16 | Analog and digital electronics | Revise circuits |
| 17–18 | Power electronics | Revise machines |
| 19–20 | Measurements | Revise power systems and control |
| 21–23 | Revision with the [formula sheet](formula-sheet.html) | One previous year paper per week |
| 24–26 | Mocks: two per week, with mistake analysis | Weak topics only |

## Tips for electrical engineering

- **Circuits and machines carry heavy weight**; get both strong before moving on.
- **Use per-unit** in every power-system calculation; it removes most transformer-ratio mistakes.
- **Phasor diagrams** solve many machines and power questions faster than algebra.
- Use official previous year papers from the organising IIT's site; see [how to analyse them](../prep/previous-year-papers.html).
