This page gives two schedules for GATE Civil Engineering: a **6-month plan** (about 4–5 hours a day) and a **3-month plan** (about 7–8 hours a day). Both use the lessons of this track, the [Engineering Mathematics](../em/index.html) and [General Aptitude](../ga/index.html) tracks, and the [mock test](mock-test-1.html).

> **Note:** GATE 2027 is organised by IIT Madras ([gate2027.iitm.ac.in](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/)). Check the official brochure for the syllabus and dates.

## Subject order

Mechanics comes first because structures, RCC and steel build on it. Fluid mechanics comes before hydrology and environmental engineering for the same reason.

1. [Engineering mechanics and solid mechanics](mechanics-solid-mechanics.html)
2. [Structural analysis](structural-analysis.html)
3. [Concrete and steel structures](concrete-steel-construction.html)
4. [Geotechnical engineering](geotechnical-engineering.html)
5. [Fluid mechanics and hydraulics](fluid-mechanics-hydraulics.html)
6. [Hydrology and irrigation](hydrology-irrigation.html)
7. [Environmental engineering](environmental-engineering.html)
8. [Transportation engineering](transportation-engineering.html)
9. [Geomatics engineering](geomatics-engineering.html)

## 6-month plan

| Weeks | Main subject | Side subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Solid mechanics | Engineering Mathematics: linear algebra, calculus |
| 4–6 | Structural analysis | Engineering Mathematics: ODEs, probability |
| 7–8 | Concrete and steel | General Aptitude: quantitative |
| 9–11 | Geotechnical engineering | Engineering Mathematics: numerical methods |
| 12–14 | Fluid mechanics and hydraulics | General Aptitude: verbal and reasoning |
| 15–16 | Hydrology and irrigation | Revise solid mechanics and structures |
| 17–18 | Environmental engineering | Revise geotechnical |
| 19–20 | Transportation and geomatics | Revise fluids |
| 21–23 | Revision with the [formula sheet](formula-sheet.html) | One previous year paper per week |
| 24–26 | Mocks: two per week, with mistake analysis | Weak topics only |

## Tips for civil engineering

- **Learn IS code values** that are in the syllabus (for example limit state factors and limiting depths), and note which code each comes from.
- **Units are a trap**: kN vs N, mm vs m, and days vs seconds in hydrology. Write units in every step.
- **Draw a free body diagram** for every mechanics and structures question before writing equations.
- Find official previous year papers on the organising IIT's website; see [how to analyse them](../prep/previous-year-papers.html).
