Two schedules for GATE Mechanical Engineering: a **6-month plan** (about 4–5 hours a day) and a **3-month plan** (about 7–8 hours a day). Both use the lessons of this track, [Engineering Mathematics](../em/index.html), [General Aptitude](../ga/index.html) and the [mock test](mock-test-1.html).

> **Note:** GATE 2027 is organised by IIT Madras ([gate2027.iitm.ac.in](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/)). Check the official brochure for the syllabus and dates.

## Subject order

Mechanics is the base for strength of materials, machines and design; thermodynamics is the base for heat transfer and its applications.

1. [Engineering mechanics and strength of materials](mechanics-strength-of-materials.html)
2. [Theory of machines and vibrations](theory-of-machines-vibrations.html)
3. [Machine design](machine-design.html)
4. [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics.html)
5. [Thermodynamics and applications](thermodynamics-applications.html)
6. [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.html)
7. [Materials and manufacturing](materials-manufacturing.html)
8. [Industrial engineering](industrial-engineering.html)

## 6-month plan

| Weeks | Main subject | Side subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Engineering mechanics, strength of materials | Engineering Mathematics: linear algebra, calculus |
| 4–5 | Theory of machines and vibrations | Engineering Mathematics: ODEs |
| 6–7 | Machine design | General Aptitude: quantitative |
| 8–10 | Fluid mechanics | Engineering Mathematics: probability, numerical methods |
| 11–13 | Thermodynamics and applications | General Aptitude: verbal and reasoning |
| 14–15 | Heat transfer | Revise SOM and TOM |
| 16–18 | Materials and manufacturing | Revise fluids and thermodynamics |
| 19–20 | Industrial engineering | Revise design |
| 21–23 | Revision with the [formula sheet](formula-sheet.html) | One previous year paper per week |
| 24–26 | Mocks: two per week, with mistake analysis | Weak topics only |

## Tips for mechanical engineering

- **Thermodynamics needs steam-table practice**: learn to read and interpolate tables quickly.
- **Manufacturing is factual and wide**: make one-page notes per process and revise them often.
- **Industrial engineering is quick marks**: EOQ, forecasting, LP and queueing questions are short.
- Use official previous year papers from the organising IIT's site; see [how to analyse them](../prep/previous-year-papers.html).
